Make That Sentence Move!

Interactive Student Song Sheet
Grades 9-12 | Richard A. Daniel, EL Teacher

1Learn the verb test

A verb shows an action, links the subject to a description, or helps another verb. First find the subject. Then ask: What does the subject do? What is the subject?

Perfect-response strategy: Read the whole sentence. Find the subject. Locate the word or verb phrase that shows action or state. Identify its type and tense. Then explain how it works in the sentence.

2Listen and follow the lyrics

First listen: enjoy the track. Second listen: notice the verb types and time clues. Open each section as the song plays.

Intro and Verse 1
Dale, Laurel, feel the motion. A noun gives the name, an adjective gives detail, But the verb makes the sentence move. Uno, dos, tres - make it move! Students read, teachers guide, ideas grow, Every complete sentence needs a verb to show An action, a feeling, a state or condition. Find the subject first, then find its position. Run, write, study - action you can see. Think, believe, remember - action mentally. Ask what the subject does or what the subject is. That question helps you prove exactly where the verb lives.
Pre-Chorus and Chorus
What happens? What is? What does the subject do? Find the verb, check the tense, use the sentence clues.
Make it move! Find the action in the line. Past, present, future - put the verb in time. Nombra el verbo, say the action loud, Match it to the subject, make the sentence proud. Action, linking, helping - every verb has work to do. If it shows what happens or what is, the verb comes through.
Verse 2 - Verb Types
Action verbs show movement: speak, create and learn. Linking verbs connect the subject: seem, become and turn. The music sounds exciting - sounds connects the two. A helping verb joins the main verb: will, have, can and do. They are studying; she has finished; we can write. Helping plus the main verb makes the meaning right.
Breakdown and Verse 3
Yesterday we studied - past tense! Today we study - present tense! Tomorrow we will study - future tense! One student writes; two students write - make the subject and verb agree! Singular subject, singular verb: the student reads. Plural subject, plural verb: the students read with speed. I am, you are, she is - be can change its face. Use the form that fits the subject, time and place. Irregular verbs may change: go became went, See became saw - use the tense you meant.
Bridge and Final Chorus
We plan the project - plan is action in that line. The plan is on the table - noun is what you find. The same word changes jobs, so check the sentence every time. Make it move! Find the action in the line. Past, present, future - put the verb in time. Action, linking, helping - choose the proper view. Match it to the subject and explain what it can do. Nombra el verbo, let the meaning come through. Dale, Laurel - make that sentence move!

6Write a strong response

Prompt: Write one sentence with a helping verb and a main verb. Then explain the job of each verb and identify the tense.

Sentence frame: The students __________ __________. The helping verb is __________. The main verb is __________. Together, they show __________ tense.

7Exit ticket

In “We plan the project,” what job does plan do?

Which sentence has correct subject-verb agreement?

Which word is the helping verb in “She has finished”?
