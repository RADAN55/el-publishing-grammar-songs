Describe That!

Interactive Student Song Sheet
Grades 9-12 | Richard A. Daniel, EL Teacher

1Learn the adjective test

An adjective describes or modifies a noun or pronoun. First find the noun. Then ask: What kind? How many? Which one?

What kind?bright planbright describes plan
How many?three notebooksthree describes notebooks
Which one?those studentsthose points to students
After a linklesson seems easyeasy describes lesson
Perfect-response strategy: Read the whole sentence. Find the noun or pronoun. Locate the word that adds detail. Ask which adjective question it answers. Then explain what word it modifies.

2Listen and follow the lyrics

First listen: enjoy the track. Second listen: notice how each adjective adds detail. Open each section as the song plays.

Intro and Verse 1
Dale, Laurel, turn the color up. A noun gives the name; an adjective gives the detail. Uno, dos, tres - describe it! A student walks in with a bright new plan, A loud bell rings and a patient teacher stands. Bright tells what kind of plan is on the way, Loud tells what kind of bell begins the day. An adjective describes a noun or pronoun, It paints a sharper picture when the details come around.
Pre-Chorus and Chorus
What kind? How many? Which one do you choose? Find the noun, ask the question, then prove it with the clues.
Describe that! Make the picture clear. What kind, how many, which one is here? Nombra el adjetivo, say the detail loud, Find the word that modifies the noun inside the crowd. Color, size, number, feeling, shape or sound, If it tells more about a noun, the adjective is found.
Verse 2 - Details and Placement
Three gold notebooks - how many and what kind? Those careful students keep the learning on their minds. Three gives the number; gold describes the look, "Those" tells which students; "careful" tells us more. Before the noun or after a linking verb, Check the job inside the sentence, not only where you heard the word.
Breakdown and Verse 3
A noisy hallway - what kind? Noisy is the adjective! Five assignments - how many? Five is the adjective! That computer - which one? That is the adjective! The lesson seems easy - easy still describes the lesson! A predicate adjective follows linking words: The music sounds exciting; the students feel prepared. Compare with -er: taller, faster, brighter in the light. Use -est for three or more: the strongest in the night. More beautiful, most important - longer words may change, Use the form that fits the meaning; keep the sentence clear and strong.
Bridge and Final Chorus
The gold shines brightly - gold is naming something there. The gold notebook is useful - now gold describes with care. The same word changes jobs, so read the sentence everywhere. Describe that! Make the picture clear. What kind, how many, which one is here? Common or proper? That's a noun's own name. The adjective adds detail - different grammar game. Color, size, number, feeling, shape or sound, If it tells more about a noun, the adjective is found. Dale, Laurel - describe that!

6Write a strong response

Prompt: Write one complete sentence with a noun and at least two adjectives. Then explain which noun the adjectives modify and what details they add.

Sentence frame: The __________, __________ __________ was __________. The adjectives __________ and __________ modify the noun __________ because they tell __________.

7Exit ticket

In “The lesson seems easy,” what job does easy do?

Which sentence contains a predicate adjective?

In “the gold notebook,” what word does gold modify?
